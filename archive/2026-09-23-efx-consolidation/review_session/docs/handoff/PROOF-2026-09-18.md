### Stage 4 — the accidental duplicate, and why it matters

The buttons were clicked twice (double-click or mail-client prefetch). The second pass returned:

```json
{"ok":true,"alreadyClosed":true,"code":"E_ALREADY_CLOSED",
 "message":"Task TK-6814B039 is already closed","requestId":"REQ-598BF070"}
```

BOSS likewise reported `alreadyAssigned: true` and made no second assignment. **The chain is
idempotent under a double click** — unplanned, but a genuinely useful result, because real managers
and email scanners will do this.

## Honest notes

- The approval gate started as an **editable form** and was simplified to a **plain approve link**,
  matching the JR pattern. The editable version worked but was more fragile and offered no reject path.
- Three faults were found only by running it, all fixed: the emailed link resolved to `localhost`
  (the n8n instance's base URL is unset); data from before the approval was unavailable afterwards;
  and the form rendered blank values because it referenced the wrong node's output.
- `Draft accounts` **invents** the SiteDocs and DSS values locally. It is a placeholder for real
  integrations, and is the one node that would have to be replaced to make the gate real.
